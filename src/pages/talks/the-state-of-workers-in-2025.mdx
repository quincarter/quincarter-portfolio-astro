# The State of Workers in 2025

This is a talk I gave 4 times in 2025. I am passionate about performance on all devices and catering to the lowest end device for an all inclusive experience on the web. This talk focuses on that as a pivotal reason for implementing workers in 2025, but you should consider workerizing as much as possible to maximize your application structure and performance.

## Slides

These are the slides I presented at RVA.js in April 2025 - only slightly modified them for my internal conferences that I spoke at for Capital One, but this breaks down quite a few key points:
- Follow RAIL
- Know your users
- Know your lowest end devices
- How to implement workers
